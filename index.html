01 — About Tasa · Est. 2017

Modern-industrial grit meets classic barbershop craft.

Reception Wall · Concrete & Wood Slats Est. 2017 · Marikina

Started on E. Santos Street in Concepcion Uno, Marikina in 2017, Tasa is a boutique barbershop built to feel like a neighborhood sanctuary.

Step past the spinning red-white-blue barber pole into raw grey cement walls, warm wood paneling, and a black ceiling lit by signature white hexagonal LED strips, black dome pendants, and vintage lantern sconces. Settle into a brown leather Chesterfield sofa beside potted snake plants before stepping into a striped black-and-white TASA cape.

Every detail at the station is purposeful—wooden shelves stacked with rolled white towels, gold clippers and shears, locally crafted chairs on chrome hydraulic bases, and barbers in crisp black-and-white uniforms.
